Pressure Pop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Pressure Pop (Android package com.camankader.pressurepop), developed and published by Abdelkader Caman Souleyman ("CAMAN KADER"), N'Djamena, Chad. It explains what information is collected when you play, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

The game itself does not ask for your name, e-mail address, or any account. It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. Your progress (levels, stars, settings) is stored only on your device.

The game includes two third-party services that may automatically collect certain technical data:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — to display advertisements (rewarded videos and interstitials). AdMob may collect the advertising ID of your device, IP address, device model and OS version, app usage data related to ads, and approximate location derived from the IP address. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.
  • GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS) — to understand how the game is played (sessions, levels started and completed, ads watched, purchases) so that we can fix bugs and improve difficulty. GameAnalytics collects a randomly generated user identifier, device model, OS version, app version, country (from IP address) and gameplay events. It does not collect your name or contact details. See the GameAnalytics privacy policy.

2. How the information is used

  • To show ads and measure their performance (AdMob).
  • To measure and improve the game: retention, difficulty balance, crash and error detection (GameAnalytics).
  • To restore your "Remove Ads" purchase, if you buy it (Google Play Billing).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use the data for any purpose other than those listed above.

3. Advertising and your choices

Ads may be personalized based on your interests, unless you opt out. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads). Where required by law (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and certain U.S. states), the game shows a consent form powered by Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is loaded; you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings screen ("Privacy options").

The optional in-app purchase "Remove Ads" disables interstitial ads permanently. Rewarded ads remain available only when you choose to watch them.

4. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will remove it. Ad requests are tagged for child-directed treatment where required.

5. Data retention and security

Data collected by AdMob and GameAnalytics is retained according to their own policies. Progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or use "Reset progress" in Settings. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights regarding data held by Google or GameAnalytics, use the links above; for anything else, contact us at the address below.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
