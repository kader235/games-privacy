Bugtopia - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy is for the Android game Bugtopia (package com.camankader.bugtopia), made by Abdelkader Caman Souleyman (CAMAN KADER), N'Djamena, Chad.

What the game collects

You don't need an account to play. The game does not ask for your name or e-mail and does not use your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. Your scores, levels, anthill progress and settings are saved on your phone only.

The game uses two outside services that can collect some technical data:

  • Google AdMob, to show ads (rewarded videos and interstitials). AdMob can collect your advertising ID, IP address, phone model, Android version and data about the ads you see. More info: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • GameAnalytics, to see how the game is played (sessions, levels started, finished or failed, ads watched) so I can fix bugs and adjust difficulty. It uses a random ID, phone model, Android version, game version and country. It does not collect your name or contact details. More info: https://gameanalytics.com/privacy/

How it is used

Only to show ads and to improve the game. I don't sell any data.

Ads and your choices

Ads can be personalized. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your phone settings (Settings > Google > Ads). In Europe, the UK and some other places, the game asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can change your answer any time in the game settings ("Privacy").

Children

The game is for everyone but is not made for children under 13. I don't knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think your child has sent personal data through the game, write to me and I will delete it.

Keeping and deleting data

AdMob and GameAnalytics keep their data according to their own policies. The progress saved on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, CCPA...), you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. For data held by Google or GameAnalytics, use the links above. For anything else, contact me.

Changes

This policy can be updated. The date at the top shows the last change.
