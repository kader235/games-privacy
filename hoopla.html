HOOPLA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game HOOPLA (Android package com.camankader.hoopla), developed and published by Abdelkader Caman Souleyman ("Caman Kader"), N'Djamena, Chad. It explains what information is collected when you play, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

The game does not ask for your name, e-mail address, or any account. It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location. Your progress (stage, hoops, upgrades) is stored only on your device.

The game is being evaluated with a publishing partner and includes third-party software development kits that may automatically collect certain technical data:

  • Voodoo TinySauce (Voodoo SAS, Paris, France) — a testing and analytics kit used to measure how the game is played during its evaluation. It relies on the services below.
  • GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS) — sessions, stages started and completed, in-game events, a randomly generated user identifier, device model, OS version, app version and country (derived from the IP address). See the GameAnalytics privacy policy.
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — app install and app-open events used to measure the performance of advertising campaigns that promote the game. It may collect the advertising ID of your device, IP address, device model and OS version. See Meta's privacy policy.

The game currently shows no advertisements and offers no in-app purchases.

2. How the information is used

  • To measure and improve the game: retention, difficulty balance, crash and error detection.
  • To measure the performance of advertising campaigns that promote the game (install attribution).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use the data for any purpose other than those listed above.

3. Your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads). Uninstalling the game stops all collection.

4. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will remove it.

5. Data retention and security

Data collected by the services above is retained according to their own policies. Progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights regarding data held by GameAnalytics or Meta, use the links above; for anything else, contact us at the address below.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
